---
description: One client's whole ATO relationship - correspondence, lodgments, payment plans, contact history - before any call or letter.
---

1. Run `npm run ato -- client "<name>"`. Partial names and family group names resolve; an ambiguous name lists candidates.
2. Read the whole card before drafting or advising anything about this client.
3. Lead with what is live: money due ahead of them, open correspondence, an overdue instalment, an overdue lodgment. Then the history.
4. If the operator is about to ring them, give the three things to say: what arrived, what it means against what we lodged, what happens next.
