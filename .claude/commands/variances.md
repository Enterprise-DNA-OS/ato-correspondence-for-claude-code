description: Every assessment that differs from what the practice lodged - the check the incumbent gates behind its top tier, run as one command.
---

1. Run `npm run ato -- variances`.
2. Every row is a decision: accept, amend or object. A row with NO EXPLANATION RECORDED is not yet decidable, and finding the reason is the first move: pull the document (`doc <ref>`), compare against the lodgment on the card, and look for the usual causes (data-matching add-backs, denied deductions, an ATO arithmetic adjustment, a missed instalment credit).
3. Record what was found: `check <ref> --note="the reason"`.
4. A variance older than 30 days is flagged by `/compliance` because objection windows under TAA 1953 Part IVC are real deadlines. If the operator wants to dispute, say the window depends on the taxpayer's circumstances, point at docs/compliance.md, and diarise a task today.
5. Never call a variance right or wrong yourself: state the numbers, the difference, and the options. Nothing here is tax advice.
